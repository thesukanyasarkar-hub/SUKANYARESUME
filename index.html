Professional Experience

Head of Operations

2022 - Present

Nmadic (Tourcompass India Pvt. Ltd.), Bengaluru

A Bengaluru-based travel-tech company with four business lines: curated holiday packages, a commission-free homestay and villa booking platform, visa and forex services, and BrilloBook, a channel manager for hotels and homestays.

  • Own end-to-end operations for holiday packages, from client enquiry and itinerary costing through supplier booking, travel-day support and post-trip follow-up
  • Coordinate between sales, clients and destination partners (DMCs, hotels, transport) across Ladakh, Sikkim–Darjeeling, Bhutan and Southeast Asia
  • Run group and family departures, including large group itineraries of 12 to 75+ travellers
  • Manage supplier relationships and negotiated rates so packages stay competitive and margins stay healthy
  • Help onboard homestay and villa owners and set up how bookings, inventory and partner communication are handled
  • Part of the founding team that raised a ₹40 lakh pre-seed round; now working on the seed round
  • Helped grow the business to ₹31 lakh in sales in FY 2024–25, with more than ₹45 lakh already booked this financial year

Operations & Partner Onboarding – BrilloBook

2025 - Present

BrilloBook.com, a Nmadic product (Tourcompass India Pvt. Ltd.)

BrilloBook is a B2B SaaS channel manager for homestays and villas. It connects properties to OTAs and bundles a free one-click property website plus zero-commission listing on Nmadic's B2B travel-agent portal.

  • Work on onboarding homestay and villa owners onto the channel manager, with OTA connectivity already live on Agoda, MakeMyTrip and EaseMyTrip
  • Support the Agoda partnership to onboard the first 100 properties, coordinating a team of 11 interns working directly with property owners
  • Help list owner inventory on Nmadic's B2B portal, reaching around 3,000 travel agents
  • Bridge owners, the tech team and OTA partners so properties go live smoothly

Founding Team – Sponsoringit

2026 - Present

Sponsoringit.com, Bengaluru (early-stage startup)

Sponsoringit is a sponsorship marketplace that matches events, clubs, artists and creators with the right brands, and connects organisers to service providers. It earns a commission on sponsorships it closes.

  • Building the startup from the ground up alongside the web platform, which is now in development
  • Partnered with BBCA on Durga Puja 2026 sponsorships under a commission-based agreement, running outreach to 116+ companies
  • Ran sponsor outreach to 50+ brands for the TGIC Assam Edition in Guwahati
  • Signed on to source sponsors for War of the Bands and Wings of Love 2 (both February 2027)
  • Working on the seed round and partnerships with event management companies
